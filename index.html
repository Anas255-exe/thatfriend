ThatFriend runs the whole loop by itself. You only step in twice: to approve the plan and to type the OTP.

Plan

Send a destination, a budget and who's coming. You get 3 itineraries with real costs, and can tweak them in plain words: "cheaper stay", "add the toy train".

Rally the squad

ThatFriend texts each friend for you, collects yes/no and requests ("veg food pls"), and updates the plan to match.

Approve once

You see one final cart with the per-person split. Tap approve, type the bank OTP, and it books everything in one checkout.

Book & split

Tickets, rooms, cabs and activities get booked, with PNRs sent to everyone. Each friend gets a UPI request for their share.

Sarvam: talks like your friends

Invites go out in each friend's language (Hindi, Tamil, 9 more) as text and Bulbul voice notes. ThatFriend even "calls" them: no telephony needed, just voice-note calls they answer by holding the mic. Replies are transcribed with Saaras, and Sarvam-105b backs up the planner.

🚩

Flagsmith: per-user controls

Voice notes, calls, translation, AI friend personas and your auto-book spend limit are feature flags, evaluated per user. Flip one in Flagsmith and the bot changes on the next message, with no redeploy.

🐝

NudgeBee: autonomy with guardrails

Checkouts above your limit go through an approval-gated runbook. If a provider goes down mid-booking, the runbook retries, fails over to an alternate provider and reports the incident.
